Read before use

  • Not a live alert system. Monthly totals, updated when the pipeline is rebuilt (latest complete month: n/a). For fires burning now, use the NASA FIRMS Fire Map or FIRMS area alerts.
  • Hotspots are not only wildfires. They include agricultural, land-clearing, peat and planned savanna burns. A high month can mean more planned burning, not a disaster.
  • Hotspots are not fires or burned area. One large fire gives many hotspots; cloud and smoke can hide fires. Counts are estimates in NOAA-20 units; see the validation error under Method.
