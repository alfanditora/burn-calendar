The question

Is this fire season worse than normal, and where?

For anyone, from a disaster agency to a student. Burn Calendar answers it for Indonesia, and for Northern Australia to show the method travels.

5.1×

VIIRS vs MODIS

The problem

NASA’s 25-year fire record is split across three sensors that count differently: VIIRS sees about 5× more hotspots than MODIS over the same land. Suomi NPP stopped on Nov 2, 2026; MODIS is retiring. Raw counts from 2005 and 2025 can’t be compared.

How it works · 28.9 million NASA FIRMS hotspots → one monthly record

1

Download FIRMS area API: MODIS, VIIRS Suomi NPP, VIIRS NOAA-20, standard + near-real-time

2

Clean confident detections; drop volcanoes, gas flares and offshore sources

3

Count inside real country and province borders, per month and 25 km square

4

Calibrate on years two sensors overlap, with a drift check for MODIS orbits

5

Validate by holding out each overlap year and predicting it

6

Analyse trend test, anomaly vs median, fire season, early signal

Benefits

  • One consistent record across the MODIS → VIIRS handover
  • Plain answers for the public; numbers with their error for analysts
  • Free, open data, any area

Honest limits

  • Hotspots are not burned area
  • Not only wildfires: farm, clearing, peat and planned burns look alike from space
  • Monthly, not live alerts
